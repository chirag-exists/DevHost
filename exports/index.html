3D Video Compositing Recommendations

Alpha Interpretation

All PNG assets use Straight (Unmatted) Alpha. When importing into After Effects or Blender, select Straight - Unmatted to avoid dark fringe outlines around shadows and glowing edges.

3D Depth Layering (Z-Space)

Base interface: Z = 0
Leg 1 & Leg 2 timeline cards: Z = -40px
Akasa recommendation card: Z = -80px
1-Click Rebook button: Z = -120px (casts soft shadow on card)
Disruption Banner: Fly in from Z = -300px with 15° X-rotation.

Camera & Depth of Field

Use a 50mm or 85mm virtual lens with subtle Depth of Field (f/2.8) to focus on the Akasa Air QP-1382 recommendation card while letting the background canvas gently blur.

Vector SVGs in 3D

SVGs can be imported directly into Blender or Cinema 4D as vector splines/curves to generate 3D extruded geometry with bevels for the ResqTravel Shield Logo and action buttons.
